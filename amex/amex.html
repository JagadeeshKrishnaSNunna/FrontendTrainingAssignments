Membership Rewards Account

Amex Offers helps Card Members earn rewards on purchases for things they love. The program gives eligible Card Members the opportunity to earn rewards typically after the Card Member makes purchases at select merchants or within specific industries (depending on the Amex Offer). Rewards can be earned in the form of Statement Credits (as a statement credit back on a purchase or as a % back in the form of a statement credit on a purchase); or Membership Rewards® points, Delta SkyMiles®, Hilton Honors Points, or Marriott Bonvoy® points on qualifying purchases. The reward is dependent on the offer and Card type.
